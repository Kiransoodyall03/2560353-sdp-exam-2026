1 Service overview
The Local Weather Monitoring (LWM) service is a network of five small weather stations (“sites”)
spread around the ARL. Topic prefix: arl/lwm.

 Entity kind           Ids                       Notes
                                                 Weather stations. Each publishes its position (lat, lon) in
 site                  lwm-1 to lwm-5            $topology and as retained state. The sites span about 6 km east to
                                                 west and 4 km north to south, 3 to 4 km apart.

1.1 General state and routines
• Sampling: every site samples every 60 s by default. The interval can be set per site from 10 s to 3600 s
  (multiples of 10 s). Mean wind, maximum gust, rainfall and strike counts aggregate the period since
  the site’s previous sample.
• Site-specific instruments: lwm-3 carries a sonic anemometer, lwm-4 a soiling station (clean and
  exposed reference solar cells), lwm-5 an evaporation pan. Each publishes an extra stream at the site’s
  sample interval.
• Power: each site runs on a 12 V battery charged by a small solar panel. Shorter sample intervals use
  more power, so a site left on a short interval overnight can run its battery flat. A site whose battery is
  exhausted goes offline until it has recharged.
• Store and forward: a site that loses its link buffers up to 720 samples (12 h at 60 s). When the link
  returns it re-publishes the buffered samples, oldest first, with their original ts and seq and backfill:
  true, interleaved with new live samples (backfill: false). Alerts are judged on live samples only.
• Calibration rota: a technician recalibrates each site every 21 to 35 days (last_calibration updates).
  Recalibration can also be requested.
• Pan refill rota: every day at 08:00 local a technician tops the lwm-5 pan up to 205 mm if it is below
  190 mm (last_refill updates).
• Lightning: each site detects strikes within 30 km. Strikes seen by at least one enabled, online site are
  published as events and summarised once a minute.
• Technician visits (service_anemometer, clean_reference_cell, refill_pan) cannot start while
  lightning is close (Lightning Proximity active) and are abandoned if it becomes active during the visit.
  A site runs at most one long-running command at a time.
• Load: about 0.15 messages/s at the default interval (5 weather, 3 site-specific, 1 lightning summary a
  minute), plus lightning events during storms; about 0.8 messages/s with every site at 10 s.

Telemetry and event payloads carry an envelope: v (schema version, integer, currently 1), ts (simulated
time, Unix milliseconds), seq (per-topic sequence number, restarts with the service; gaps reveal lost
messages) and entity (entity id). The measurement fields are added next to the envelope. A
measurement that cannot be taken is null. State payloads are {"v", "ts", "entity", "item",
"value"}.

Commands are JSON {"command_id": <string>, "researcher": <researcher code>, "value":
<value>}. The researcher must be verified by the ARL API for the service. Acknowledgements carry
command_id, target, control, researcher, state (accepted, rejected, in_progress, completed,
failed), progress (0 to 1 for long-running controls), reason and detail. Rejection and failure reasons:
controls_disabled, malformed, unknown_control, unknown_target, unknown_researcher, researcher_
defunct, researcher_not_permitted, api_unavailable, invalid_value, precondition_failed, busy
(the target is already running a long command), fault, timeout, interrupted.
Alerts are events on arl/lwm/events/alert with alert_id, type (the alert key below), severity (info,
warning, critical), state (raised or cleared), value, limit, unit, message and metadata. An alert
that escalates is raised again with the higher severity under the same alert_id. Limits come from the
ARL API (/v1/alert-limits/ + lwm).

5 Configuration
The service reads a TOML file (config/ + lwm.toml in the bundle, --config or ARL_CONFIG for a binary).
Environment variables override the file, and command-line options override both (see Running the ARL).
Defaults are the values in the provided file.
